What It Is

Pump (ticker: PUMP) is the native token of Pump.fun, a Solana-based memecoin launchpad and decentralized exchange platform. The token contract address is `pumpCmXqMfrsAkQ5r49WcJnRayYRqmXz6ae8H7H9Dfn`, deployed on the Solana blockchain with 6 decimals, and it is the primary asset of the `pump-fun` listing on CoinGecko.

Pump.fun is categorized across multiple segments including Decentralized Exchange (DEX), Exchange-based Tokens, SocialFi, DeFi, Automated Market Maker (AMM), Solana Ecosystem, Launchpad, Meme, and CoinList Launchpad. Its core product is a permissionless token launch platform where users can create and trade memecoins, with the platform capturing fees on activity and routing a portion toward token buybacks (community reports cite a 50% of net revenue buyback model).

Key characteristics from the data:

Primary DEX Pair: The deepest tracked pair is PUMP/USDC on PumpSwap (`2uF4Xh61rDwxnG9woyxsVQP7zuA6kLFpb3NvNRQeoiSd`), with approximately $23.4M in liquidity.
Official Links: Website `https://pump.fun`, X/Twitter `@pumpfun` (CoinGecko lists `@pumpdotfun`), and GitHub `github.com/pump-fun`.
Market Position: Market cap rank #51 (rank #64 including rehypothecated supply), with roughly 67,972 CoinGecko watchlist users.
Supply Structure: Total supply ~825.5B, max supply 1T, circulating supply ~460.7B — meaning roughly 46% of max supply is circulating, which is reflected in the market-cap-to-FDV ratio of 0.56.

1. How Much of the On-Chain Buy Skew Is the Buyback Program?
Pump.fun routes 50% of net protocol revenue into buying back $PUMP on the open market and immediately burning it via a programmatic smart contract.
Volume Share: At reported revenue levels ($772k to $1M+ daily buyback allocation), if the protocol executes even half of these purchases through its native PumpSwap PUMP/USDC pool, protocol buying accounts for 25% to 40%+ of that pool's entire $2.92M daily volume.
Transaction Count Share: Automated buyback bots rarely place a single daily market order. Instead, they execute sliced market orders (TWAP or fee-threshold triggers) every few minutes or blocks. As a result, protocol buybacks can easily account for 60% to 80%+ of the total buy transaction count on PumpSwap over any given 1-hour window.
The DEX pool's 237/37 transaction ratio primarily reflects the protocol systematically consuming liquidity to burn tokens, while organic traders are net-distributing on CEXs and derivatives desks.
2. How to Distinguish Protocol Buybacks from Organic Demand On-Chain
To filter out protocol activity and isolate true organic demand, on-chain analysts look for four distinct technical signatures:
#### A. Wallet & Smart Contract Signers
Protocol Buybacks: Transactions originate from known, static protocol fee-sweeper/treasury addresses (e.g., Pump.fun's execution hot wallet `3vkpy5...s3WTi` or designated Multisigs). The transaction instructions invoke automated execution routines (like Jupiter/PumpSwap routing contracts linked directly to the fee vault).
Organic Demand: Trades originate from a wide distribution of unique Solana pubkeys with diverse transaction histories and non-zero interactions across other DeFi/NFT protocols.
#### B. Trade Size & Order Uniformity
Protocol Buybacks: Display high mathematical uniformity. Orders are either structured as identical USDC/SOL amounts (e.g., exactly $500 or $1,000 per tranche) or are dynamically pegged to exact percentage sweeps of protocol fee accumulation.
Organic Demand: Follows a power-law distribution—a chaotic mix of micro retail buys ($10–$200), variable bot swaps, and sporadic large whale market orders.
#### C. Execution Cadence & Trigger Mechanics
Protocol Buybacks: Highly periodic or deterministic. Orders execute on fixed time intervals (e.g., every 5, 10, or 15 minutes) or trigger automatically as soon as the protocol fee vault hits a programmatic balance threshold.
Organic Demand: Stochastic (randomly distributed in time), tightly clustered around price breakouts, social media news events, or overall Solana/Bitcoin momentum shifts.
#### D. Immediate Post-Trade Token Destination (Atomic Burn vs. Wallet Accumulation)
Protocol Buybacks: The purchased $PUMP tokens never sit in a standard wallet. Under the April 2026 programmatic model, the buy transaction is atomically bundled with a transfer to the Solana Incinerator / dead address (`11111111111111111111111111111111`) or a protocol burn instruction.
Organic Demand: Tokens land in individual user SPL token accounts, where they remain, get transferred to cold storage, or are deposited into centralized exchanges (like the 300M PUMP exchange deposit mentioned in the report).
Summary Conclusion
The 1-hour buy skew on PumpSwap is an operational flow indicator, not a market sentiment indicator. It confirms that Pump.fun’s revenue-funded buy-and-burn engine is actively functioning, but when weighed against a 0.78× perp taker ratio, $130M+ in outside CEX volume, and exchange deposit inflows, organic market participants are net sellers.
